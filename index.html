WHO WE ARE 01 / ABOUT ABN

A network built on collective progress.

Ambedkarite Business Network (ABN) is a community-focused initiative of Bahujan Samaj Vikas Sansthan (BSVS), designed to help businesses, customers, and service providers find one another and thrive.

We believe visibility, meaningful connections, and access to opportunity can help local enterprise reach further.
